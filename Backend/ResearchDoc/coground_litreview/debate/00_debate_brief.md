Debate question:

Rules:
- Argue from your assigned stance, but stay evidence-bound: cite specific papers from the agent files (or ones you verify by web search; mark unverified ones). Never invent papers or numbers.
- Do not assume CoGround is novel or that AR is better. Distinguish "nobody built it" from "nobody tested it" from "tested, with null result".
- Keep the three concepts separate: Task Interpretation / Plan Feasibility / Goal Satisfaction.
- Be concrete: each claimed novelty must be a one-sentence claim + the closest prior work that threatens it + why it survives (or not) + what study/feature would secure it.
- Output: a position paper (≤ 900 words, English) with sections: Stance summary (3 lines) · Novelty claims ranked (table: claim | type [interaction/system/empirical/design-knowledge] | closest threat | strength High/Med/Low) · What to investigate next (ranked, concrete) · Biggest risk · Questions to the other debaters.
